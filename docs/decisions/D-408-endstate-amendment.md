Round 3 (the Natural Language Control Plane): the vision buried its potentially defining pillar inside the 100x table's NLCL row as "implementation cleanup." Corpus evidence proves otherwise: the vivim-next MASTER-SPEC's defining properties #1 and #2 are "NLP, NLCL, and LLM escalation are deterministic, composable tiers — not monolithic magic" and "Natural language reprogrammability ... a closed mutation grammar"; the MASTER-BLUEPRINT intent-signal table puts capability-system at 377 signals — the single highest topic of 3,225 mined intent statements (above providers 241, ui-ux 202, conversation 174, agent-execution 103); the old tree carries ActionPlan IR (K0-03), Execution Kernel (K0-02), Consent Engine (K0-09), a CapabilityResolutionEngine, and a first-party ContextAssemblyEngine.
