## Decision

**Decision:** (a) Supersede explicitly — the owner's verbatim directive above
is the call. In substance:

- **§28 row 3** of the vision doc is amended to read: *Chrome master/slave
  (`provider.browser`) is the shippable-v1 substrate — and the healing loop's
  proving ground; no AI-API realization ships in v1.* The `FRESH / DEFER`
  verdict stands: what defers is the AI-API realization, to a post-v1 stage.
- **§24's Frank scenario** Ollama badge is re-scoped, not silently struck:
  `provider.llm-ollama` is explicitly re-dated to a **post-v1 stage** and the
  passage says so in place.
- **§31's Wave-1 row** vertical-slice list is rewritten to name
  `provider.browser` in place of `provider.llm-ollama` as the W5 atom's
  realization boundary.
- **`docs/ROADMAP.md`'s existing supersede banner** gains the one-line pointer
  prescribed by the course correction: the Ollama-first W2 sequencing below is
  additionally superseded by this record — see here for the v1 substrate call.
- **`docs/forge/OMEGA-FORGE-ARCHITECTURE.md`** gains the same banner coverage
  (its round-one boundary table row 4 and its "Ollama over ChatGPT" rationale
  are sequencing-era prose, markered as superseded, table cell amended to
  `provider.browser` for the boundary the Wave-1 atom now realizes through).
- **`docs/migration/20-WAVES/WAVE2-PROVIDER-STRATUM.md`** gains a banner line
  (its order line teaches "Ollama first" as W2 mechanics; the wave doc is the
  roadmap's named authority for mechanics, so it gets the pointer, not a
  rewrite).
- **`docs/forge/annex/OMEGA-CONSOLIDATION-INTEGRATION.md` §4.10** — the
  carried ship-posture claim gains its decision pointer: decided here.
- **Untouched, deliberately, as historical record:** `provider.llm`'s code
  (simulator-mode, gated live leg), D-338/D-380/D-381 (their Ollama-candidacy
  lines document the engineering of their era and are cited as history by
  this record), the migration assessment/triage corpus's harvest notes
  (T-02's "ollama pilot candidate" is a note about legacy manifests — legacy
  VIVIM is frozen and is not design input), and the four internal proving
  compositions that carry `provider.llm` (fenced separately by D-420's
  mechanical gate, not by this record).
